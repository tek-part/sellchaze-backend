Merchant catalog feeds and Google Merchant information
=====================================================

The private product-feed and product-feed/download endpoints support Google,
Meta, Snapchat and TikTok. They share store.settings.manage authorization with
Google Merchant settings. Public feeds resolve the active store by its own host
at /api/v1/storefront/product-feed/{channel}; unknown/suspended stores and unknown
channels cannot export. Both public and private downloads are throttled and use
no-store headers. Export and information requests do not mutate stock or orders.
There are no provider calls, OAuth tokens or advertising-account writes here.

Google uses UTF-8 tab-separated data; the other channels use UTF-8 CSV, with a BOM
and CRLF records. TikTok uses sku_id; the other feeds use id. Public catalog data
is projected explicitly; cost, supplier identifiers, delivery links/codes, private
customer data and tracking credentials are excluded. Text is stripped of HTML and
controls, CSV fields are quoted, and leading spreadsheet formula characters are
escaped. Cover/gallery images must have safe HTTP(S) URLs and a supported file
extension. TikTok accepts JPG/PNG; the other feeds also accept WebP. Unusable images
and missing required brand/GTIN data for social feeds omit the row. No fake brand,
MPN or GTIN is synthesized. GTIN checks lengths/check digits and rejects all zeroes.
Actual image dimensions, external accessibility and provider moderation remain
the merchant's responsibility and require production acceptance.

Each active variant is exported separately, with its own price/image/availability
and ?variant=ID landing URL. The parent ID is item_group_id. Standalone product IDs
stay numeric; variant IDs are p{product}-v{variant}, shared with cart/checkout and
purchase advertising items. Generic parent views use product_group matching.
Reserved physical stock and available digital codes determine availability.
Prices use the base currency and current one-unit offer; compare prices become
regular price plus sale_price. Draft/future products and inactive variants are
excluded. Existing products with no publication timestamp remain supported.

Apply 2026_10_12_060000_add_google_merchant_configuration BEFORE code activation.
It adds one NULL JSON column to stores. Existing stores remain disabled. The
private google-merchant GET/PUT endpoints require a shared version and reject stale
edits. Enabling requires contact details and authored shipping/return policies;
the authored COD policy may be disabled independently. Formatting is sanitized by
the existing product/page HTML policy. Nothing is generated as a legal document.
Disabling unpublishes contacts/policies without deleting authored draft content.
Down refuses any populated configuration; production rollback must preserve it.

Enabled information is exposed deliberately through merchant_information in the
public bootstrap/render context, with automatic policy footer links. Existing
store contact fields, custom pages, publications, themes and menu records are not
rewritten. The raw configuration is hidden from normal Store serialization.

Validation: nine dedicated feature tests/145 assertions cover formats, tenant and
permission guards, safe data projection, price/stock freshness, real variant order
and browser/server/catalog ID matching, stale edits, publication/sanitization and
populated rollback and unresolvable product slugs. Full local baseline852/7050
passed before the final phone/slug guards; final dedicated tests and PHPStan/Pint
passed afterward. Exact release CI is required.
Actual local browser downloads were parsed and matched Blue200/available and
Red350/unavailable; the browser download-event observer timed out, but actual
created files were independently verified. No live provider upload was attempted.
