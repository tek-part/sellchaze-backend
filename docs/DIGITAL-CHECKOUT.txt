Digital and mixed checkout — 2026-10-10

Scope: shipping/contact/payment eligibility for physical and digital baskets.
Digital fulfillment as a whole and the full competitor rebuild remain in progress.

Authoritative requirements:
  - Any physical line requires the store's configured shipping fields/selections.
  - A basket of link/code products alone has no address, delivery method or fee.
  - Any digital line forces a visible required email, including bank transfer.
  - Client requires_shipping/price fields do not control these decisions.

CheckoutBasket inspects tenant-owned active products. Form and payment endpoints
accept product_ids[] (at most100); unknown/foreign IDs fail without leaking values.
Requests with submitted items use those IDs; server-cart integrations inspect the
customer and guest carts together, matching the later merge, without creating or
merging carts during validation. Missing/invalid baskets retain physical defaults
until item validation rejects them. Actual order placement locks the cart and
resolves its live products again, rechecking digital email and physical address
rules before saving the order. Product digital type is immutable.

GET /storefront/checkout/fields includes requires_shipping, has_digital and each
field's digital_required flag. A pure digital basket gets disabled empty shipping
configuration. GET /storefront/payment-methods uses the same IDs to exclude COD
from pure digital baskets; mixed baskets retain COD. POST checkout independently
rejects pure-digital COD and does not synthesize a COD fallback for that basket.
When no eligible gateway is enabled, no order/cart is created. Bank transfer remains
unpaid until a separate confirmation flow is implemented; selection is not payment.

Quote, order and server-cart coupon totals skip shipping only for digital-only
baskets. Discounted-subtotal tax (inclusive and exclusive) remains unchanged.
Pure digital orders persist shipping_address=null, even if stale address data is
submitted. Mixed orders retain the owned region/option snapshot and normal charges.
Digital snapshots remain hidden while unpaid; this pass adds no email or code release.

Frontend: cart and direct funnel forms query by stable product-ID basket keys,
reload payment eligibility when that basket changes, and hide the shipping total
row only after the authoritative quote identifies a digital-only order.
New cart additions preserve digital type; all five drawers omit free-shipping
progress for entirely digital lines. Legacy cached lines without type retain
physical presentation until refreshed; checkout is always server-derived.
Fixed a separate cart-row bug: the horizontal name link no longer inherits the
whole-card pseudo-element overlay which intercepted remove/quantity controls.
The direct funnel also uses the current theme surface/text/border tokens instead
of obsolete variables and white fallbacks which made dark-mode text unreadable.

No new migration is needed for this pass. Production archive/dist is preserved;
all source builds go to audit/commerce-build and changes are local, undeployed.

Remaining digital requirements: guest receipt, manual bank confirmation, email
delivery, authenticated provider acceptance, digital/mixed B2B/carrier handling,
shared code-pool client cart caps, remaining all-theme/mobile/dark verification and MySQL
concurrency. No full digital feature or full competitor parity completion claimed.
