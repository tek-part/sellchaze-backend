Product preferences and catalog visibility

Apply 2026_10_16_020000_add_product_preferences before deploying the paired frontend.
Existing rows retain nullable theme overrides, zero display priority and catalog visibility.
Configured preferences, priorities or hidden flags prevent a destructive migration rollback.

Merchant saves accept only validated preference keys. Updates supplying preferences,
display_priority or catalog_hidden require the current preferences_version. Stock and ordinary
price updates do not invalidate this version. Portable JSON imports/exports and duplicates
retain preferences; exported versions are never reused for another product identity.

Higher display_priority sorts first in default product ordering. Existing position and ID ties
remain intact; explicit customer sorts retain their own order. Catalog-hidden active products
retain direct purchase links but leave public lists, active category counts, sitemap and feeds.
Review visibility does not delete stored reviews. Disabling it also prevents new submissions.

Product free shipping applies when every physical product in a checkout qualifies. Digital
items do not incur shipping. A mixed physical basket retains its delivery fee. Existing quantity
offer free shipping and order-wide thresholds continue to work. Accepted orders snapshot the
delivery result and free product IDs; later preference changes cannot reprice an old replay.

pixel_price is private configuration: add-to-cart and enabled product-surface checkout use it
only for advertising values. Normal checkout, order lines, invoices, inventory and business
metrics retain actual amounts. Consent, DNT/GPC, idempotency and delivery safeguards remain.
Inclusive tax is not added twice to advertising gross values. No outbound provider call occurs
within checkout acceptance. Simulated visitor/stock/countdown displays do not alter inventory.

The paired storefront shares product purchase sections across native theme families. Direct
selection is store/product-scoped, expires in thirty minutes and retains no client prices.
Direct and same-page checkout preserve the ordinary basket and use existing payment recovery,
delivery, minimum-order, phone verification and bot protection contracts. Dedicated product
landing-page authoring is a separate workflow and remains under competitor review.
