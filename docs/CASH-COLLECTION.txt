Cash on delivery collection

POST /api/my-store/orders/{order}/payment/confirm-cash (also available under
the existing /stores/{store} scope) requires store.orders.manage and is limited
to 20 requests per minute. The tenant-bound route rejects foreign order IDs.

The merchant reviews a collection reference, the full immutable grand total,
the currency, an explicit collected acknowledgement and an optional internal
note. Decimal amounts accept up to two decimal places, without exponent
notation. Partial, overpaid, mismatched currency, cancelled, non-COD and
inconsistent payment states are rejected. Legacy COD orders without a matching
pending transaction fail closed; this endpoint does not create replacement
transactions or infer a payment from shipping events.

ManualCashPayment locks the COD transaction before the order, matching payment
callbacks. Payment updates, the actor/time/reference audit entry, digital paid
outbox event and B2B customer payment synchronization share one transaction.
Failure rolls them all back. A matching paid reference and cash metadata makes
an identical retry a no-op; a different reference or inconsistent paid state
is rejected. The original audit and note remain unchanged on replay.

Physical delivery remains independent of collection. A delivered parcel does
not release digital values. Confirming collection does not change order or
shipment status, consume physical stock, transfer money or update a wallet.
B2B stores the full customer payment, while its quantity includes physical
lines only. Internal notes and transaction metadata are not returned through
the buyer receipt. The private receipt and paid email expose digital values
only after confirmed payment.

The merchant UI captures a review payload and prevents concurrent clicks.
After a network/5xx uncertainty it retains the same reference for retry or
offers a status refresh. Responsive Arabic/English copy explains collection
and digital release. Pending COD buyer receipts and receipt emails explain
that delivery alone is not confirmation of payment.

Validation: CashCollectionTest covers real synthetic checkout, carrier
delivery without payment, receipt/code privacy, authorization/tenant isolation,
exact amounts, duplicate/conflicting references, physical inventory stability,
malformed metadata, audit actor, rollback and array-mail publication. A guarded
local SQLite browser order also verifies a stale second-tab replay, a single
audit/outbox event, one paid array email and repeat publication of zero.

No schema migration or live provider action is introduced. Actual provider,
SMTP and simultaneous MySQL acceptance remain separate deployment checks.
Transport success does not prove buyer inbox delivery; SMTP retries after a
missing database acknowledgement may duplicate mail. Partial collection,
refunds and returns are outside this endpoint. Full competitor parity and
editable source parity with captured production remain unfinished.
